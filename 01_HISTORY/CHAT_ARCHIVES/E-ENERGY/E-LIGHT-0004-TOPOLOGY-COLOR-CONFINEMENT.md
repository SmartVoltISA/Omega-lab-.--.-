# E-LIGHT-0004 — Topology, color-mode and confinement

Date: 2026-08-23
Status: exploratory toy-model result; not physical validation.

## Hypothesis
A relation can be treated as a state-bearing object. Color labels are operational modes:
- RED: dispersive/repulsive tendency (chaos if unconstrained)
- GREEN: neutral/balanced state
- BLUE: joining/attractive tendency

Topology/geometry may constrain the available directions of restructuring. Confinement therefore may preserve an active potential without eliminating it.

## Experiment A — RED repulsion
2D particles with pairwise repulsive interaction were simulated in two conditions: open space and circular confinement.

One representative run (N=40):
- open: mean radius from origin ≈ 1.99, maximum radius ≈ 2.89
- circular confinement: mean radius ≈ 1.06, maximum radius ≈ 1.33

Interpretation: the boundary strongly reduces spatial dispersion. It does NOT mean energy disappears; it means available motion is constrained.

## Experiment B — BLUE joining tendency with boundary-like restoring field
A second toy system added a weak inward restoring interaction representing a BLUE surrounding relation, while retaining weak internal repulsion.
Representative run (N=40):
- free control mean radius ≈ 1.95, radial spread ≈ 0.73
- BLUE-boundary condition mean radius ≈ 1.26, radial spread ≈ 0.47

Interpretation: a surrounding joining field can reduce dispersion and preserve a more compact configuration.

## Important limitations
1. These are phenomenological toy models, not simulations of electrons, photons, or quantum electrodynamics.
2. The RED/BLUE force rules were explicitly defined, so the observed behavior is expected from the model and cannot be called a discovery of nature.
3. The experiment supports only the structural statement: topology/boundaries can convert an unconstrained dynamic tendency into a constrained persistent state.
4. No claim is made that physical colors themselves are fundamental interaction types.

## Next test
Replace the imposed RED/BLUE forces with a common interaction law and let the observed state be encoded afterward. Then introduce an optical-frequency-like driving term and test whether frequency selects different relation transitions at equal injected energy.

## Working chain
relation → state → interaction → restructuring → topology constraint → persistent state → information-bearing relation.
