# Ω-Lab Research Status

This document is a compact map of the current research state.

## 1. Ω-0 — Time and memory

**Question:** what minimum structure is required for internally distinguishable order and functional memory without assuming a physical time coordinate?

**Preliminary result:** an updating retained trace can create an internal before/after distinction in the tested minimal construction.

**Status:** promising first result, not yet independently reproducible from archived source code and seeds.

**Next:** Ω-MEM — characterize the minimum functional memory.

## 2. Ω-B — Internal dynamics

The original hypothesis concerned self-organizing domains and "self-will".

Controls showed:

- multiple domains are strongly dependent on the chosen diffusion rule;
- the diffusion rule therefore created an architectural artifact;
- the original "self-will" interpretation is not established;
- internal chaotic dynamics were reported to differ from equivalent white noise;
- the mechanism behind that difference remains unresolved;
- the spatial-shuffle and stronger matched-null controls remain important.

**Status:** hypothesis narrowed substantially; useful candidate effect remains under investigation.

## 3. Methodological rules

Ω-Lab follows these rules:

1. Do not treat an attractive visualization as evidence by itself.
2. Separate data, observation, interpretation and hypothesis.
3. Fix metrics and thresholds before inspecting final results when possible.
4. Preserve negative results and detected artifacts.
5. Compare against null models and conventional models.
6. Archive code, parameters, seeds and raw outputs for reproducibility.
7. Never rename an unexplained effect as a mechanism.
8. Do not protect an Ω hypothesis from falsification.
