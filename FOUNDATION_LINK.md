# FOUNDATION LINK

OMEGA-LAB is the experimental/research layer. It consumes Foundation axioms and is responsible for disciplined experiments and evidence.

Primary bindings:
`STATE → TRANSITION → PROVENANCE → VERIFICATION → HISTORY → FEEDBACK → RECOVERY`

Experimental status boundaries remain mandatory:
`HYPOTHESIS ≠ PROPOSED ≠ EXECUTED ≠ RESULT ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ CANONICAL`

No experiment becomes a Foundation axiom without explicit reconciliation and promotion.
