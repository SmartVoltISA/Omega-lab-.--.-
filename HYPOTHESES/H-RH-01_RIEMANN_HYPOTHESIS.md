## 1. Question

Can the Riemann Hypothesis be resolved by a new, independently checkable mathematical route, including a possible connection to the Ω-Lab foundation architecture?

The project does **not** assume that the Ω-Lab foundation explains the Riemann Hypothesis. Any connection must emerge from mathematics and survive independent verification.

## 2. Exact target

For the Riemann zeta function

`ζ(s) = Σ n^(-s)`

initially defined for `Re(s) > 1` and then analytically continued, the hypothesis states:

> Every non-trivial zero of `ζ(s)` has real part `1/2`.

Equivalently:

`ζ(ρ) = 0  and  0 < Re(ρ) < 1  ⇒  Re(ρ) = 1/2.`

## 3. Research discipline

This track follows the Ω-Lab evidence rules:

- proposed ≠ tested;
- tested ≠ proved;
- numerical agreement ≠ proof;
- unknown ≠ false;
- negative results are preserved;

- existing mathematics is checked before a new construction is proposed;
- any claimed proof must be independently auditable line by line.
