# Ω — Relation Collapse / Attractor Hypothesis

Date: 2026-08-13
Status: HYPOTHESIS / TO BE TESTED

## Origin

During review of prior relation/edge experiments, a distinction was proposed between two possible outcomes of relation dynamics:

1. **Attractor** — relations converge toward a stable organization while relevant distinctions remain present.
2. **Collapse** — relations converge so strongly that the distinctions represented by the system disappear or become degenerate.

This document records the hypothesis only. It does not redefine the previously recorded experimental classifications.

## Working picture

`relations → interaction → redistribution → convergence`

The convergence may lead to different regimes:

`convergence → stable distinguishable organization → attractor`

or

`convergence → loss of distinguishability → collapse`

A third possibility remains open:

`convergence → continuing redistribution / cycle`

## Core question

Are attractor and collapse genuinely different dynamical outcomes of the same relation process, distinguished by what happens to relation-level distinguishability?

## What must be checked

Use the existing experiment(s) that already produced attractor and collapse cases. Do not replace them with a new toy model unless required.

For representative runs of each outcome, inspect the relation state at:

1. initial state;
2. intermediate evolution;
3. immediately before classification;
4. final/classified state.

Measure or visualize:

- number of distinguishable relations;
- relation strengths/weights;
- concentration of relation values;
- number of distinct relation configurations;
- persistence of differences;
- convergence rate;
- whether multiple relations become effectively identical;
- whether a stable configuration retains internal differentiation.

## Candidate interpretation

The hypothesis predicts that an attractor should preserve some nontrivial relational differentiation, whereas collapse should drive the system toward a degenerate relational configuration.

This is NOT yet established.

## Important distinction

Do not equate:

`convergence = collapse`

or

`stable = attractor`

without inspecting the actual relation state.

A system may converge while retaining structure. A system may also continue moving in a bounded recurrent regime.

## Relation-first constraint

Ω does not assume nodes as primitives. The analysis must begin with relations and their interactions. Any node/structure interpretation is secondary and must emerge from the relation state if supported.

## Existing-history anchor

Relevant prior material includes the recorded relational-dynamics work and the existing edge-only results. The current task is to analyze those results at the relation level rather than create an unrelated new ontology.

## Falsification

The hypothesis is weakened if attractor and collapse cannot be distinguished by any reproducible relation-level observable and are merely labels for the same relational state.

It is strengthened only if a reproducible difference is found and survives controls.

## Honesty rule

No confirmation is claimed until the original experiment is reproduced and the relation trajectories are actually inspected.

`Execution not verified. No experimental result claimed.`
