# Ω-MEDIUM-002 — Electron Hunt: emergent dual structures

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: PILOT RESULT / ELECTRON NOT IDENTIFIED

## Question
Can an electron-like object be found by growing relations from a minimal medium rather than inserting electron properties in advance?

## Method
A minimal 1D three-state local medium was searched over randomly sampled local transition rules. No electron, charge, mass, spin, electromagnetic field, or particle labels were supplied.

A candidate rule was selected only after observing localized periodic propagating structures from the raw dynamics.

Candidate local rule table (neighborhood encoding 000..222):

[1,1,1,2,1,2,0,1,0,1,2,2,0,0,1,1,2,0,1,0,1,0,0,2,0,2,0]

## Emergent structures

Two distinct localized configurations emerged:

A = [2,1], period 2, displacement +1 per period.
B = [1,2], period 2, displacement -1 per period.

Thus the same medium generated two structurally related, oppositely propagating localized modes.

## Collision

A and B were initialized on opposite sides and allowed to approach. Before contact both retained their localized two-cell forms. During collision the two structures did not simply pass through unchanged: the configuration changed and subsequently settled into a single propagating [2,1]-type structure.

This is a genuine interaction effect of the medium, not an externally imposed collision rule.

## Interpretation

The result establishes a stronger emergent hierarchy than the previous binary pilot:

entity seed → interaction → relation → localized structure → propagation → interaction between structures → changed structure.

The two modes can be described as polarity-like partners only as a descriptive analogy. They are NOT identified with electron/positron and no physical charge has been demonstrated.

## Critical controls / limitations

1. The rule space was sampled, not exhaustively searched.
2. The medium is a discrete 1D cellular automaton, not physical space.
3. No conservation law equivalent to electric charge has yet been established.
4. The collision produced one surviving localized mode rather than clean annihilation to vacuum.
5. No inverse-square field, mass-energy relation, spin-1/2 analogue, quantum amplitude, or Lorentz symmetry has been demonstrated.
6. Therefore the electron has NOT been found.

## Next search target

Do not add electron properties. Search the same minimal medium for a rule/structure satisfying, in order:

C. two symmetry-related modes;

Only after these emerge should the resulting object be compared with known electron properties.
