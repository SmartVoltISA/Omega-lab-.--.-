# Ω Company — Founders Control Policy v1.0

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL BUSINESS RULE  
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Purpose

This document records the founders' control principle for the future Ω Company. Investment is intended to finance development and scaling, not to transfer control of the company or its strategic direction.

## Core rules

1. **Founders retain at least 51% of voting control.**
2. **Strategic decision-making remains under founders' control.**
3. Investment does not by itself grant an investor control over the company.
4. Transfer of core intellectual property, change of control, liquidation, or other defined reserved matters require founders' consent.
5. The company's constitutional documents and shareholders'/founders' agreement must implement these principles in legally enforceable form.
6. The exact legal mechanism depends on the jurisdiction and corporate structure and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before execution.

## Principle

> **Capital is brought into the company to develop the foundation, not to acquire control of the foundation or the company.**

## Governance target

The intended governance state is:

```text
FOUNDERS >= 51% voting control
          |
          +--> strategic decisions
          +--> core IP protection
          +--> change-of-control protection
          +--> long-term company direction

INVESTORS --> capital / expertise / network / market access
             without automatic transfer of control
```

This is a business architecture principle, not a substitute for legal advice or executed corporate documents.
