# Ω — Information as relational organization

**Status:** OPEN / experimental definition
**Introduced:** 2026-08-13

## Question

Can a measurable part of information be located in the organization of elements and relations rather than in the multiset of elements alone?

## Working distinction

Ω does not define information as a single universal quantity at this stage.

For this research line we distinguish:

- **Element content** — which symbols/elements exist and in what multiplicity.
- **Relational organization** — how elements are ordered or connected.
- **Structural information** — measurable properties that depend on that organization and can change while element content is held constant.

The distinction is operational, not metaphysical.

## Minimal intervention

Given a sequence `X`:

1. preserve its length;
2. preserve every element and its multiplicity;
3. change only the ordering/relations;
4. compare predefined structural metrics.

If a metric changes under this intervention, the metric is sensitive to organization rather than to element counts alone.

This does **not** by itself prove that information is a physical substance located in relations. It establishes only a measurable distinction between composition and organization.

## Current hypothesis H-INF-1

> When element composition is held constant, changing relational organization can produce a measurable change in structural information.

**Status:** OPEN. Not confirmed by definition.

## Falsification condition

H-INF-1 should be weakened or rejected for a tested representation if controlled reordering does not produce reproducible differences in any preregistered organization-sensitive metric, or if the observed differences are fully explained by an uncontrolled change in composition, length, encoding, or another confound.

## Required separation

Every result must distinguish:

- DATA — exact sequence and transformation;
- OBSERVATION — measured difference;
- INTERPRETATION — possible meaning;
- HYPOTHESIS — claim requiring further testing.

## Next research levels

`Ω-INF-1` tests character-order organization.

Later experiments should separately test:

- word order;
- sentence order;
- local versus long-range relations;
- preservation of n-grams under higher-order permutations;
- graph representations of the same information;
- recovery of the original structure from relational traces.

No higher-level conclusion should be imported into the lower-level experiment.
