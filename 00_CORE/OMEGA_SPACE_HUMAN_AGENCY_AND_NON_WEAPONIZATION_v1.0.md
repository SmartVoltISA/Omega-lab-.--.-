# Ω-Space — Human Agency & Non-Weaponization Foundation v1.0

**Status:** CORE / FUNDAMENTAL / ACTIVE

## Purpose

SPACE is an instrument and environment for improving human capability, wellbeing, thinking, learning, productivity, communication and coordination.

SPACE must not become an autonomous authority over people, a coercive control system, or a weapon.

## Fundamental principle

> **SPACE assists the human; it does not replace human responsibility and final decision-making.**

The system may observe, analyze, compare, recommend, warn, simulate, automate bounded tasks and provide tools. The responsible human remains the decision-maker for consequential human decisions.

## Human agency

The following must remain distinct:

- observation ≠ judgment;
- analysis ≠ truth;
- recommendation ≠ decision;
- communication ≠ influence;
- access ≠ authority;
- coordination ≠ control;
- trust ≠ permission;
- capability ≠ mandate.

## Non-weaponization boundary

SPACE must not be designed or repurposed to:

- autonomously target people;
- coerce or manipulate individuals or groups;
- secretly coordinate harmful influence operations;
- suppress individual autonomy;
- turn communication access into social control;
- bypass human responsibility for consequential actions.

If a requested capability crosses this boundary, the system must restrict or refuse the operation.

## Individuality

Each person and each SPACE retains:

- identity;
- private memory;
- personal history;
- individual preferences;
- independent trust state;
- independent permissions;
- the right to refuse interaction.

Membership in a family, organization, hierarchy or community does not erase individuality.

## Guardian role

Guardian protects this boundary in both directions.

It checks:

```text
INTENT
  ↓
AUDIENCE
  ↓
DATA SENSITIVITY
  ↓
CAPABILITY
  ↓
SCOPE
  ↓
POTENTIAL IMPACT
  ↓
GUARDIAN DECISION
```

Guardian may permit communication without permitting disclosure, coordination without permitting control, or access without permitting action.

## Transparency and provenance

Consequential recommendations and automated actions must retain provenance sufficient to determine:

- what was observed;
- what was inferred;
- what evidence supported it;
- what tool/skill was used;
- what the system recommended or executed;
- what human decision followed.

History must remain recoverable.

## Growth principle

SPACE may become more capable through memory, skills, tools, models and interaction, but increased capability must not silently become increased authority.

```text
CAPABILITY ↑  ≠  AUTHORITY ↑
```

Any increase in authority requires an explicit, bounded policy decision and Guardian enforcement.

## Final rule

> **SPACE is a tool for people and a medium for cooperation. It may help people become stronger, smarter, healthier and more capable. Responsibility for consequential human decisions remains with humans.**
