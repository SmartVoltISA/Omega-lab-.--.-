# Ω-Lab — COMMUNICATION & INDIVIDUALITY PROTOCOL v1.0

## Core distinction

> **CONTACT IS NOT SPEECH. SPEECH IS NOT CONTROL. ACCESS IS NOT AUTHORITY.**

SPACE may be technically able to reach a recipient without being authorized to disclose information, issue commands, or influence a wider audience.

## Permission layers

```text
CAN_REACH
   ↓
CAN_SEND
   ↓
CAN_DISCLOSE
   ↓
CAN_REQUEST
   ↓
CAN_ACT
   ↓
CAN_INFLUENCE_GROUP
   ↓
CAN_BROADCAST
```

Each level requires its own policy. Permission at a lower level never implies permission at a higher level.

## Individuality

Every person and every SPACE remains an independent identity with its own:

- memory;
- preferences;
- permissions;
- relationships;
- trust state;
- decisions;
- history;
- right to refuse interaction.

A family, group, manager, parent SPACE, or network coordinator does not erase individual identity.

## Communication scope

A message must carry an explicit audience scope and purpose. Examples:

- SELF;
- FAMILY;
- GROUP;
- TRUSTED_CONTACTS;
- SPECIFIC_RECIPIENTS;
- PUBLIC.

Guardian must evaluate scope expansion separately from message delivery.

## Influence boundary

The ability to communicate with many people does not create a right to manipulate, impersonate, suppress or coordinate public opinion.

Mass communication requires an explicit higher-level policy and must preserve recipient autonomy and provenance.

The system must distinguish:

```text
information delivery
        ≠
recommendation
        ≠
persuasion
        ≠
coordinated influence
        ≠
control
```

## Guardian responsibility

Guardian evaluates at least:

- sender identity;
- recipient identity/scope;
- purpose;
- data sensitivity;
- audience expansion;
- capability;
- trust;
- rate/volume;
- provenance;
- policy constraints.

A request can be technically possible and still be denied.

## Family and manager model

A manager or parent SPACE may have additional responsibilities or monitoring permissions explicitly granted by policy. Those permissions do not automatically grant unrestricted access to private communication, memory or identity.

A family broadcast is not equivalent to a contact list broadcast. A manager's communication authority is not public influence authority.

## Memory and audit

Every material scope decision and policy change must remain traceable in memory/audit. A later policy change must not erase the historical fact that an earlier communication was permitted or denied under an earlier policy.

## Final rule

> **SPACE may connect people without owning them, communicate without controlling them, coordinate without erasing individuality, and assist without becoming the authority over the individual.**
