## 2. Exact target

RH is equivalent to the statement that all non-trivial zeros of ζ(s) satisfy Re(s)=1/2. Equivalently, all zeros of the completed Xi function on the corresponding real variable are real.

## 3. Jensen–Pólya target

For the Taylor-coefficient sequence γ(n),

J_d,n(X) = Σ_{j=0}^d C(d,j) γ(n+j) X^j.

Pólya's criterion gives an equivalence: RH holds iff the relevant Jensen polynomials are hyperbolic for all degrees d and shifts n. Therefore a genuine proof must establish the infinite statement, not merely many numerical cases.

## 4. Finite computation audit

A direct floating-point moment implementation was attempted using the Fourier-kernel representation. It produced apparent non-hyperbolic Jensen polynomials at higher shifts/degrees.

AUDIT RESULT: these apparent failures are NOT accepted as mathematical counterexamples.

Reason: Jensen-polynomial root calculations become severely ill-conditioned as degree/shift increase; the kernel-moment calculation and root extraction were not performed with a certified arbitrary-precision error bound. A false numerical counterexample is therefore possible.

This explicitly supersedes the earlier informal statement that d=4 showed a problem. That observation was a numerical artifact candidate, not a verified counterexample.
