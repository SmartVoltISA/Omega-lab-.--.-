# RH-43 — Proof Acceptance Gate

Date: 2026-08-26

## Objective

Define exactly what is required before Ω may mark the Riemann Hypothesis as SOLVED.

## Target

RH: every nontrivial zero rho of zeta satisfies Re(rho)=1/2.

Equivalent target routes may be used, but the final implication to RH must be explicit and unconditional.

## Acceptance gate

A proof is accepted only if every item below is established from stated definitions and previously proved theorems.

1. **Exact object** — the function/operator/kernel used is exactly linked to the completed zeta function or to a theorem known equivalent to RH.
2. **Domain and regularity** — all domains, convergence, differentiability, integrability, self-adjointness, boundedness/closedness claims are proved where used.
3. **Global statement** — no finite truncation, sampled interval, fitted asymptotic, numerical regression, or empirical certificate may replace an all-domain theorem.
4. **Core inequality/factorization** — establish the required inequality for every admissible test function, or an exact equivalent global statement.
5. **Limit passage** — if finite approximants are used, prove the required convergence (operator, quadratic-form, or another rigorous mode) and preservation of the needed inequality.
6. **Tail control** — every omitted tail has an explicit rigorous bound; numerical evidence alone is insufficient.
7. **No hidden hypothesis** — every lemma used is proved or cited in a theorem whose assumptions are verified in the present setting.
8. **Final equivalence** — explicitly derive RH from the established statement. No appeal to “strong evidence”, spectral resemblance, or numerical agreement.
9. **Independent audit** — the complete chain must be reproducible and internally checked; any unresolved gap means STATUS != SOLVED.

## Status rule

- SOLVED: all nine gates pass.
- BLOCKED: at least one gate remains unproved.
- DISPROVED: a rigorous negative witness establishes the negation of RH.
- EXPERIMENTAL: numerical/finite evidence only.
