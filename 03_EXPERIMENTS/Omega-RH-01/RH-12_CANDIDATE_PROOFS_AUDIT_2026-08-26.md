# Ω-RH-12 — Candidate proofs audit

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: OPEN / NO PROOF CLAIMED

## Scope

Audit the strongest current 2026 claims of a proof of RH, especially operator/determinant and Weil-positivity routes.

## Findings

1. Yoshinori Shimizu v8 (July 2026) constructs a self-adjoint Hilbert–Schmidt determinant model and claims FK(s) ≡ ξ(s). The paper is explicitly marked NOT PEER-REVIEWED. Earlier versions contain explicit fatal-error disclaimers; therefore the current claim cannot be accepted without independently verifying every comparison identity and the global determinant identification.

2. Simon Velez 2026 Zenodo versions claim non-negativity of the Weil quadratic form via semilocal trace / positive spectral factorization. These are preprints/records, not an independently verified resolution of the Millennium problem.

3. Independent 2026 results materially advance finite Weil-form/inertia methods and establish that more than two thirds of zeta zeros are simple and on the critical line, but this is still far from 100% and does not prove RH.

## Load-bearing requirement

Any claimed proof must establish, without assuming RH in disguise:

  self-adjoint operator/determinant
  + exact identity with ξ(s)
  + global domain/completeness and convergence
  => every nontrivial zero lies on Re(s)=1/2.

The exact determinant identity FK ≡ ξ is the critical bridge. A local or finite-window identity is insufficient.

## Ω conclusion

No accepted proof found. The correct research state remains OPEN. Candidate proofs are research targets, not established solutions.
