# Ω-RH-01 — FIRST ATTACK / BARRIER

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Working question

Can the real-zero problem for `Ξ(t)` be forced by a structural property of its Fourier kernel rather than by checking zeros individually?

## Known reduction

`RH ⇔ all zeros of Ξ(t)=ξ(1/2+it) are real.`

The function Ξ has an even Fourier-type representation with an explicit rapidly decaying kernel. This makes positivity and operator-theoretic routes natural candidates.

## Immediate warning

Positivity of a Fourier kernel alone is not sufficient in general to imply that its Fourier transform has only real zeros. A stronger property is required (for example, a rigorously established total-positivity / Laguerre–Pólya mechanism, or a self-adjoint spectral realization).

Therefore the first attack is **not** accepted merely because the kernel is positive or numerically well behaved.

## Current result

The spectral route remains viable as a research direction, but no operator has yet been derived here whose self-adjointness and exact spectral correspondence with ζ are both proved.

## Decision

Do not claim a solution.

Next: derive the exact kernel representation, inspect its structural positivity and zero-preserving properties, and compare every proposed implication with known theorems before constructing a new Ω object.
