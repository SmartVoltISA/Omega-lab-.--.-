**Date:** 2026-08-26

## 1. New external result checked

A new 2026 preprint by Michel Planat and Patrick Solé proves the long-standing second-level concavity conjecture for the Riemann Xi kernel.

With

` s(t) = Φ(√t) `

and

` f(t) = s'(t)^2 - s(t)s''(t) `,

the result establishes

` (log f(t))'' < 0 ` for `t > 0`.

This is a genuine mathematical advance, not a numerical observation.

## 2. Why this matters

Csordas–Dimitrov showed that the combination of logarithmic concavity of the original kernel and this second-level concavity yields the double Turán inequalities for the associated Riemann Xi coefficients.

Therefore an old open intermediate problem is now closed:

`second-level concavity`
        `↓`
`double Turán inequalities`

The double Turán inequalities are necessary consequences of Laguerre–Pólya structure, but they are not by themselves a proof of RH.

The missing arrow is the research target.

## 4. Ω candidate: recursive Laguerre hierarchy

Define recursively, when the quantities remain positive,

`L_0[s] = s`

`L_{k+1}[s] = (L_k[s]')^2 - L_k[s] L_k[s]''`.

The second-level result establishes strict logarithmic concavity of `L_1[s]` for the Riemann kernel.

This suggests testing the stronger statement:

> **Recursive Laguerre hierarchy hypothesis:** for the Riemann Xi kernel, every successive positive Laguerre transform remains logarithmically concave on `(0,∞)`.

This is ONLY A HYPOTHESIS. It has not been proved here and must not be presented as fact.

## 5. Why this route is worth testing

If a theorem can be found or proved showing that a suitable infinite hierarchy of strict Laguerre inequalities generated by the kernel implies membership of the Fourier transform in the Laguerre–Pólya class, the route could potentially close the RH gap.

However, finite levels are insufficient. We must explicitly determine whether an infinite hierarchy is equivalent to, merely necessary for, or strictly weaker than RH.
