# Ω-RH-01 — RH-09 FULL LINE RESULT
Date: 2026-08-26
Status: NO PROOF / NO COUNTEREXAMPLE

## Full-line audit
The current line was re-evaluated as a chain rather than as isolated numerical tests:

Xi kernel → first/second Laguerre inequalities → Turán-type inequalities → Jensen hyperbolicity → PF∞ / Laguerre–Pólya → de Bruijn–Newman Lambda=0 → RH.

## Result
The first and second local/finite positivity statements do not close the infinite implication. In particular, second-level concavity gives useful double-Turán inequalities, but it does not by itself imply PF∞ or that Xi belongs to the Laguerre–Pólya class.

The Gershon route remains unverified. Its load-bearing infinite-region estimates and spectral-tail control have not been independently established without assumptions equivalent to the target positivity.

## New 2026 evidence checked
A recent paper proves that more than two thirds of the nontrivial zeta zeros are simple and lie on the critical line using a finite-compression Weil-form/rank-trace argument, with formal verification. This is a substantial unconditional result but is not RH because it does not reach 100%.

A separate August 2026 paper proves second-level concavity of the Riemann Xi kernel and the associated double Turan inequalities, explicitly making no RH claim.

## Conclusion
No valid proof of RH was obtained in this full pass.
No counterexample was obtained.
The strongest surviving structural directions are:
1. finite-compression Weil/Hankel positivity and an inertia argument;
2. a genuinely self-adjoint spectral operator;
3. Li/Jensen criteria with a mechanism controlling the full infinite family.

## Integrity rule
Numerical agreement, verified finite cases, or a published unreviewed proof claim are not promoted to THEOREM. Any future closure must provide a non-circular argument covering the infinite case.
