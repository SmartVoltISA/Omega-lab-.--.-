## Time–energy hypothesis
Working hypothesis:

Energy manifestation is the temporal observable of structural change in relations.

Important correction: this is a proposed unifying description, not a replacement for the physical definition of energy. In established physics, energy is a conserved quantity associated with dynamics/symmetries in appropriate systems; the present Ω formulation asks whether many observed manifestations can be usefully represented as changes of relational structure evolving in time.

Movement should therefore be treated more precisely as:

change of relational configuration with time.

It is not literally identical to symmetry. A static balanced configuration can be symmetric; motion is the temporal evolution of configuration. Symmetry may constrain or generate conservation laws, while motion is the observed evolution.

## Symmetry / cloud example
Two opposite charge domains can form a globally balanced configuration. A snapshot of + and − separated charge is not itself a moving process; it is a state/configuration. The subsequent redistribution, discharge, or field evolution is the temporal process.

Therefore:

BALANCED CONFIGURATION ≠ NO ENERGY
BALANCED CONFIGURATION ≠ NO POTENTIAL
ZERO OBSERVABLE ≠ ZERO SYSTEM STATE

The RLC control confirms the last point: when one energy storage channel reaches zero, the other can hold the energy. See E-AC-0002.

## Memory
Memory is hypothesized to be representable as persistence of relational state/history rather than requiring a separate storage object:

RELATION(t+1) = F(INPUT(t), RELATION(t), NEIGHBORHOOD, HISTORY)

A neutral observable can therefore contain latent potential/history.

## Topology
Topology is an active constraint, not passive geometry.

Open chain → more available escape/reconfiguration paths.
Closed loop → constrained paths and possible circulation.
Cell/closed boundary → confinement of rearrangement.
Tree → branching without loops.
Graph with cycles → multiple alternative paths and feedback.

A topology can therefore change the accessible set of transitions without changing the nominal input energy.

## Electricity
AC control:
V(t) = V0 sin(ωt)

The mean of V over a cycle can be zero while power transfer is nonzero in a load.

RLC control:
U_C = q²/(2C)
U_L = Li²/2
P_R = Ri²

For R=0, U_C + U_L is conserved and exchanges between electric and magnetic storage. For R>0, stored energy decays through dissipation.

This supports:

ZERO OF ONE STATE VARIABLE ≠ ZERO OF TOTAL POTENTIAL/ENERGY

It does not prove that energy is nothing more than link rearrangement.

## Lightning working chain
Observed physical process to compare against the relational model:

charge separation
→ electric field growth
→ ionization / leader development
→ branching paths
→ connection / conducting channel
→ rapid discharge
→ channel persistence / possible subsequent strokes

Model correspondence under test:

separation → stored potential → threshold → competing paths → successful connecting path → high-conductivity channel → redistribution → modified network memory.

This is an analogy to be tested, not an identification of the mechanisms.

## Light working chain
Photon frequency and delivered energy are separate control dimensions.

Frequency can select transitions because photon energy depends on frequency and physical systems have discrete/structured transitions. E-LIGHT-0007 toy model confirms spectral selectivity at model level and history-dependent response, but not the RGB triad as a fundamental law.

Working test:
E_total controlled + frequency varied → measure transition probability, topology/state change, and persistence.
