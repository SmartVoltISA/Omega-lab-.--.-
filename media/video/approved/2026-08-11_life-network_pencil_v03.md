# Life-Network → Pencil v03

**Status:** visual prototype / approved direction

## Core sequence

`connection → node → graph → structure → form → physical object`

## Intent

Show a physical object emerging from organization of a network rather than appearing as a pre-drawn illustration.

## Sequence

1. Individual connections appear.
2. Nodes appear on the connections.
3. The network becomes a recognizable graph.
4. Graph elements reorganize into a dense structure.
5. The structure elongates and resolves into the geometry of a real pencil.
6. The structure becomes material: wood, graphite, ferrule, eraser and surface hatching.
7. The finished pencil remains physically stable while the camera provides the visual movement.

## Physical constraints

- Pencil should be long and thin, approximately 175 mm × 7 mm in real-world proportion.
- Classic hexagonal body.
- Graphite core remains centered.
- Sharpened wooden tip.
- Metal ferrule and eraser at the rear.
- No arbitrary thickening of the body.

## Camera rule

The pencil itself should not rotate or stretch to simulate camera motion.

Preferred behavior: the camera moves around / reframes the fixed object, using perspective and a controlled push-in.

## Revision history

- v01 — first structural-to-object attempt.
- v02 — improved assembly logic; object proportions still too thick.
- v03 — corrected long/thin geometry and moved apparent rotation into camera/framing.

## Next target: v04

Make the self-organization more physical: individual network components should visibly travel along connections, cluster, lock into local structures, and become the pencil's material layers. Preserve the fixed-object/camera-motion rule.
