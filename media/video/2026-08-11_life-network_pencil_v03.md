# Life-Network → Pencil v03

## Concept

Show a physical object emerging from organization:

`relation → node → graph → structure → form → object`

## Visual requirement

The pencil must be physically proportioned as a real thin graphite pencil, not a thick block.

Target reference: approximately 175 mm length and approximately 7 mm body diameter, giving a length-to-diameter ratio near 25:1.

## Animation requirement

The graph reorganizes into the object's geometry. The object itself must not rotate or stretch artificially.

Camera movement creates the perception of viewpoint change. The camera may push in and move laterally/orbit around the fixed object while preserving the pencil's geometry.

## Materialization

The network remains visible during formation and gradually becomes:

- graphite core;
- wood body;
- ferrule;
- eraser;
- surface hatching/grain.

## Status

Concept prototype. V03 corrected the earlier object-rotation problem and improved physical proportions. Further work should use a true 3D camera/object model for convincing parallax and a realistic hexagonal pencil cross-section.
