# E-LIGHT-0005 — Spectrum as information carried by a relation

## Status
Exploratory / toy model. Not a physical derivation.

## Question
If total optical energy is held fixed, can changing photon frequency select different relational states without manually assigning red/green/blue as state labels?

## Model
Three allowed transition gaps were used only as a minimal abstraction: 1.91, 2.38 and 2.76 eV. Photon energy follows E = hc/lambda. Absorption selectivity is represented by a narrow resonance around each gap. Total incident optical energy is held fixed; photon count therefore changes with photon energy.

## Probe wavelengths
650 nm -> 1.908 eV -> state 1 selected
520 nm -> 2.385 eV -> state 2 selected
450 nm -> 2.755 eV -> state 3 selected

The same total optical energy therefore produces different state-selection patterns when frequency changes.

## Interpretation
This supports a limited structural statement: energy magnitude and spectral information can be separated. Energy controls available excitation budget; frequency can select which allowed transition is addressed. The state of the relation can therefore carry information about the interaction history.

## Important limitation
The three transition gaps and resonance width were chosen by the experimenter. Therefore this is not evidence that nature has three fundamental colour states, nor that red/green/blue are fundamental relational states. It is a control showing that frequency-dependent selection can emerge without assigning colour directly to the output state.

## Next test
Replace the hand-picked three-level system with a continuous/random network of allowed transition energies. Do not assign colours or state labels. Illuminate with a continuous spectrum and cluster the resulting relational states. Test whether stable classes emerge and whether they correlate with spectral bands.
